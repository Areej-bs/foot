==================================================
1. APPLICATION CONCEPT
==================================================

AcademyScore is a football academy and football competition platform.

There are 4 types of users:

1. Public visitor
2. Coach
3. Player
4. Referee

Public visitors do NOT need to log in.

Coaches, Players and Referees have separate dashboards.

The public experience should feel similar to a modern football live-score application such as Flashscore.

The coach interface should feel like a football academy management and player-performance platform.

The referee interface should feel like a professional match-control application designed to be used on a smartphone during a match.
